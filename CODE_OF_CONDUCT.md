# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Tinlance SDEA project a welcoming, respectful and professional experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respectful and inclusive language, constructive feedback, criticism focused on ideas and code rather than people, and respect for project boundaries and maintainer decisions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would make participation unsafe or unprofessional.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate them.

For private conduct reports or enforcement concerns, contact **hello@tinlance.com** with the subject `SDEA Code of Conduct`. Reports will be reviewed by a project maintainer.

We will make reasonable efforts to acknowledge a report within 2 business days and provide an initial response or next-step communication within 7 calendar days.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, issue and pull-request discussions, and other spaces when an individual is representing the project or its maintainers.

# Tinlance SDEA\n\n**Signal-Driven Engineering Acquisition (SDEA)** is Tinlance's permanent shared intelligence layer for detecting emerging organizational capability demand, reasoning over evidence, identifying why demand is emerging now, and determining appropriate capability-acquisition paths.\n\n**Core pipeline:** Signal → Evidence → Fusion → Demand → Capability → Opportunity → Buying Window → Acquisition Path\n\n## Why SDEA exists\n\nOrganizations continuously emit signals that reveal capability demand before they explicitly ask a vendor for help:\n- hiring and team expansion\n- funding and financing events\n- product launches and changes\n- geographic or market expansion\n- technology migrations\n- AI adoption\n- security events\n- infrastructure changes\n- leadership changes\n- regulatory and compliance requirements\n- partnerships and acquisitions\n- procurement and RFP activity\n- open-source activity\n- pricing and packaging changes\n\nA job posting is one signal type, not the definition of SDEA.\n\n## Evidence discipline\n\nSDEA separates facts, interpretation, and commercial action:\n\n```\nsignal ≠ evidence\nevidence ≠ demand\ndemand ≠ capability\ncapability ≠ opportunity\nopportunity ≠ customer\n```\n\nConsequential inferences must remain traceable to supporting evidence. Uncertainty, contradiction, partial information and unknown states are first-class concerns. Model output is not ground truth.\n\n## Canonical pipeline\n\n```\nExternal World\n     ↓\nEntity / Event\n     ↓\nSignal\n     ↓\nEvidence + Provenance\n     ↓\nSignal Cluster\n     ↓\nDemand Hypothesis\n     ↓\nCapability Need\n     ↓\nOpportunity\n     ├── confidence\n     ├── evidence\n     ├── why now\n     ├── buying window\n     └── acquisition paths\n     ↓\nAcquisition Recommendation\n     ↓\nHuman-approved commercial action\n```\n\n## Acquisition modes\n\n| Mode | Meaning |\n|---|---|\n| Hire | Add internal employees |\n| Build | Develop the capability internally |\n| Buy | Purchase an external product/platform |\n| Partner | Work with an external specialist |\n| Fractional | Use fractional expertise |\n| FDE | Embedded/fractional delivery engineering |\n| Project | Bounded implementation |\n| Managed service | Continuous outsourced operation |\n| AaaS | AI workforce / agent service |\n| Product | Tinlance product |\n\n## Permanent system boundary\n\nSDEA is a permanent first-class repository. TADS is a consumer of SDEA, not its parent.\n\n| System | Owns |\n|---|---|\n| **SDEA** | Signals, evidence, provenance, fusion, temporal reasoning, demand/capability inference, opportunity intelligence and acquisition recommendations |\n| **TADS** | Demand-intelligence product experience and workflows |\n| **ReconOS** | Account/company reconnaissance and enrichment |\n| **FadeReach** | Engagement and outreach |\n| **FDSE / FDE** | Engineering execution and delivery |\n| **Agent Platform** | Identity, authorization, approvals, runtime, tools, sandbox, budgets, evidence, audit and observability |\n| **Agent OS** | Agent/workspace lifecycle and operating environment |\n\nSDEA must integrate through explicit contracts and must not duplicate downstream authority.\n\n```\nTADS / ReconOS / Domain Adapters\n              ↓\n             SDEA\n              ↓\n         Opportunity\n              ↓\n          FadeReach\n              ↓\n      FDE / AaaS / Product\n              ↓\n        Tinlance execution\n```\n\n## Repository structure\n\n```\ntinlance-sdea/\n├── .github/workflows/ci.yml\n├── docs/\n│   ├── adr/\n│   ├── architecture.md\n│   ├── domain-model.md\n│   └── roadmap.md\n├── schemas/\n│   ├── domain/\n│   └── events/\n├── src/tinlance_sdea/\n│   ├── domain/\n│   ├── evidence/\n│   ├── signals/\n│   ├── fusion/\n│   ├── temporal/\n│   ├── inference/\n│   ├── opportunity/\n│   ├── acquisition/\n│   ├── policy/\n│   ├── evaluation/\n│   └── integrations/\n├── tests/\n│   ├── unit/\n│   ├── contract/\n│   ├── integration/\n│   └── fixtures/\n├── pyproject.toml\n├── .pre-commit-config.yaml\n└── README.md\n```\n\nThe future packages are architectural boundaries; they are not a reason to implement every subsystem immediately.\n\n## Domain contracts\n\nThe first canonical contracts are:\n- Evidence\n- Signal\n- SignalCluster\n- DemandHypothesis\n- CapabilityNeed\n- BuyingWindow\n- Opportunity\n- AcquisitionRecommendation\n\nThe contracts deliberately keep observation, inference and action separate.\n\n## Engineering invariants\n\n1. **Evidence before inference.** Important conclusions need supporting evidence.\n2. **Preserve uncertainty.** Never silently convert unknown into false or true.\n3. **No hidden authority.** SDEA does not authorize outreach, production changes or tool execution.\n4. **Recommendation is not action.** Human approval and downstream governance remain separate.\n5. **Stable contracts, replaceable intelligence.** Inference implementations may evolve behind stable domain interfaces.\n6. **Temporal awareness.** Emerging, active, stale, delayed and decaying demand must be distinguishable.\n7. **Outcome-oriented measurement.** Optimize evidence-backed opportunities and business outcomes, not raw signal volume.\n8. **No architecture duplication.** SDEA does not recreate TADS, ReconOS, FadeReach, FDSE, Agent Platform or Agent OS.\n\n## Current status\n\n**M0 — Foundation**\n\nImplemented:\n- permanent SDEA repository boundary\n- Python package foundation\n- canonical domain contracts\n- acquisition-mode taxonomy\n- domain invariants\n- architecture/domain documentation\n- CI quality baseline\n- linting and formatting\n- strict type checking\n- coverage gate\n\nNext:\n1. signal normalization contracts\n2. source adapter interfaces\n3. provenance model\n4. deduplication\n5. signal fusion\n6. temporal reasoning\n7. demand inference\n8. capability ontology\n9. opportunity graph\n10.
